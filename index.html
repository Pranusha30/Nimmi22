Clinical Specializations

Areas of Expertise

Comprehensive clinical services specializing in women’s health, lactation consultation, neonatal physiotherapy, labour delivery preparation for normal delivery, and musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

Musculoskeletal & Orthopedics

Treatment for acute and chronic conditions affecting joints, muscles, tendons, and ligaments including arthritis, frozen shoulder, bursitis, and tendonitis.

Joint Mobilization Soft Tissue Release Spine Health

Lactation Consultation

Compassionate clinical support for breastfeeding mothers: comprehensive latch assessment, nursing positioning, manual relief for engorgement and blocked milk ducts, mastitis management, and nurturing maternal-infant bonding.

Latch Evaluation Engorgement Relief Feeding Posture Duct Clearance

Post-Surgical Recovery

Structured progressive protocols following orthopedic surgeries such as total hip/knee arthroplasty, ligament reconstructions, and spinal fusion procedures.

Arthroplasty Rehab Scar Mobilization Gait Re-education

Spine Care & Posture

Conservative care for cervical and lumbar disc pathology, sciatica, mechanical lower back pain, postural syndromes, and neck tension.

Core Stability Lumbar McKenzie Posture Correction

Neonatal Physiotherapy

Specialized physical therapy for newborns and early infants. Focuses on congenital muscular torticollis correction, plagiocephaly prevention, gentle neurodevelopmental positioning, muscle tone balance, and early sensory-motor milestone stimulation.

Torticollis Therapy Developmental Milestones Gentle Positioning Infant Stimulation

Gyneic Physio & Labour Delivery

Specialized obstetric and gynecological physiotherapy to prepare expectant mothers for a safe, empowered normal delivery. Includes antenatal exercises to induce and facilitate normal vaginal birth, pelvic biomechanics, breathing techniques, and postpartum recovery.

Normal Delivery Exercises Labour Induction Movements Pelvic Floor Conditioning Postpartum Recovery
